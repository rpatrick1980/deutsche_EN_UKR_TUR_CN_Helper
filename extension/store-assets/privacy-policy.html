German Reading Helper — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Summary: German Reading Helper does not track your browsing, does not run automatically, and does not send data to any server we operate. The only data leaving your device is the German text you explicitly choose to translate or explain, which is sent directly to OpenAI.

What the extension does

When you select German text on a webpage and choose Translate or Explain Grammar from the right-click menu, the selected text is sent to the OpenAI API using the API key you provide, to generate a translation or grammar explanation. This happens only on your explicit action.

Data we collect

None on our servers. We operate no backend and receive no data.

Data stored on your device

  • Your settings (API key, enabled languages, theme, panel preferences).
  • A local history of recent lookups (auto-expires; default 30 days).
  • A local cache of recent results (auto-expires after 7 days).

All of this is stored via Chrome's local storage on your computer and can be cleared any time from the extension's Settings page or by removing the extension.

Data sent to third parties

The text you select is sent to OpenAI only when you trigger a Translate or Explain Grammar action, in order to produce the result. Your use of OpenAI is governed by OpenAI's privacy policy at openai.com/policies/privacy-policy. No other third parties receive any data.

Permissions

  • contextMenus — adds the right-click actions.
  • storage — saves your settings, history and cache locally.
  • activeTab — acts on the tab where you invoked the action.
  • scripting — shows a "please reload" notice when needed.
  • api.openai.com host access — to call the OpenAI API directly.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing support channel.
